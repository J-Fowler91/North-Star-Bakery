Freshly Baked for Our Community

North Star Bakery creates handmade breads, pastries, and cakes using carefully selected ingredients and time-tested baking methods. We welcome neighbors looking for a morning pastry, a fresh loaf for dinner, or a special dessert for an upcoming celebration.

What We’re Known For

Customers visit us for crusty artisan breads, rotating pastries, celebration cakes, and friendly neighborhood service. Our small-batch approach helps us keep the selection fresh throughout the day.
