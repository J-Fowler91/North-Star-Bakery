Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on warm service, handmade food, and the simple pleasure of sharing something fresh from the oven. Our goal is to create dependable favorites while also offering seasonal items that give customers something new to discover.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose ingredients with freshness, consistency, and flavor in mind. Whenever practical, we work with regional suppliers and use seasonal ingredients so our menu feels connected to the community and the time of year.

Meet the Team

Our bakers begin early each morning preparing breads and pastries in small batches. Front-of-house team members help customers choose everyday favorites, plan preorders, and find options that fit special occasions.

How We Bake

We value careful preparation, quality ingredients, and small-batch baking. Our breads and pastries are prepared with attention to flavor, texture, and freshness.
